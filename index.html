The Journey Behind the Work

Chapter I: The Foundation of Rigor

My professional story began back in 2015 when I earned my Bachelor of Science in Information Technology from Quezon City Polytechnic University. Fresh out of college, I stepped into my On-the-Job Training (OJT) at Asia Alliance Mining Resources Corporation (AAMRC) as a data encoder. That experience instilled in me a fundamental truth that has guided my career ever since: absolute precision and data integrity are non-negotiable.

Chapter II: Keeping Systems Alive

Eager to dive deeper into technical infrastructure, my first corporate role took me to Bonifacio Global City (BGC) at EZ Sistemo Integration Technology Corporation as a Support Tech. Stationed right in the heart of fast-paced operations, my core mission was monitoring the daily status of automated parking systems. Whenever anomalies occurred, I deployed rapid-response troubleshooting to guarantee uninterrupted daily traffic flow and system reliability.

Chapter III: The "Field Machine" Era

Following that, I embarked on a rigorous 5-year tenure as a Field Technician for FBM Technology Solutions, a premier bingo machine manufacturer. This era of my life was defined by motion. Assigned across diverse cities and provinces nationwide, I traveled constantly to ensure that deployed gaming terminals remained fully operational. From diagnosing hardware failures and managing complex system prize configurations to meticulous maintenance—including component-level motherboard cleaning and repairs—I lived on the frontline of hardware troubleshooting.

Chapter IV: Settling Roots and Finding My Niche

Seeking a new chapter, I decided to relocate and settle down in the peaceful province of Negros Occidental. It was here that my path converged with CAD drafting. I realized that my technical IT background, combined with a sharp eye for visual layouts, made technical design an instinctive fit. What started as an exploration quickly evolved into the core professional niche where I truly thrive today.

Chapter V: The Flow State & Personal Anchors

"Designing isn't just work for me; it is a deep state of flow. When I lock into a project, I get completely indulged in the moment—which is why high-speed, flawless execution comes naturally."

While past recommendations and elite delivery speeds speak volumes about my technical work ethic, my personal life provides the grounding balance. I find deep fulfillment in rewarding my milestones through meaningful life chapters—symbolized by collecting custom tattoos that mark significant milestones of personal growth. Back at home, I am happily married, sharing my daily life with my cats, who provide endless emotional grounding and daily joy.
